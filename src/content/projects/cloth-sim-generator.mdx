## Context

My final-year project for the BSc at Trinity. Machine-learning models that predict how cloth falls need a lot of physically correct examples to learn from, and there is no way to film enough of them. These two scripts generate the data instead: a body, an animation, a simulation, repeated until you have a dataset.

## What I built

- **Blender script:** creates a random SMPL body shape, retargets a skeletal animation onto it and exports the animated body, male, female and neutral.
- **Houdini script:** walks every exported body folder, loads the geometry, runs the cloth simulation and writes the result back beside it.
- A naming scheme (animation + index + gender) so any simulation can be traced back to its inputs.

## Result

A dataset of 600 simulations from 100 body shapes in 6 animations, split 500 / 20 / 80 into training, validation and test.

Computer Vision module assignment. A Mask R-CNN pre-trained on COCO was fine-tuned on 3,000 labelled images for 6 epochs (about 4.5 hours on a GTX 970). A custom PyTorch Dataset builds bounding boxes from the segmentation masks and randomly flips training images. On one test image it found a strawberry smaller than 10×10 pixels.

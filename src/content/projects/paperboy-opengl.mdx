## What I built

- Model and mesh classes, so imported models are made of meshes that can be moved relative to the whole model.
- Model loading with Assimp. I modelled the bike, houses, post boxes, street and hedges in Blender and exported them as .obj.
- Gameplay classes for the paperboy and newspapers, using the original controls: WASD to steer, Q and E to throw left and right.
- Lighting, fog, a sky sphere and texturing.

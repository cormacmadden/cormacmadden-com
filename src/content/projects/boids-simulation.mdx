Built for the Computer Animation module of the MSc. Each seal steers by three rules applied to the neighbours it can see: keep apart, match their heading, and move towards the middle of the group. Nothing choreographs the flock. The shoaling, splitting around the ice and re-forming behind it all fall out of those rules running per-agent, every frame.

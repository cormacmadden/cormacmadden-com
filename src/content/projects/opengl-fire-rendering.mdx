## What I built

- **Billboarding:** the fire is drawn on a plane that always faces the camera. The shader updates position and scale but not rotation.
- **Texture atlas animation:** the frames sit in one texture and play in a loop, so it's a single draw call.
- **Depth and lighting:** layered sprites give an illusion of depth, a flickering orange light ties the fire into the scene, and additive blending brightens the semi-transparent parts.
- **Controls:** a GUI with sliders for X/Y scale and animation speed, and toggles for the light, blending and layers.
